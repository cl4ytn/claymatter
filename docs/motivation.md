Purpose of the project:

Since I got into Computer Science, I watched as my peers developed their own projects and portfolios while I questioned what I could possibly make. This was not from a lack of confidence in my ability, but understanding in what I was passionate about. Over the years, I came across inspiring projects that I followed on youtube like a black hole simulation by k... and building an LLM from scratch by GreenCode. My biggest problem wasn't just, what do I build, but really, how do I engineer a solution to one or another of my problems. 

It wasn't until someone told me that they had designed an LLM to be able to answer questions about their work that I realized my number one problem is I don't have a portfolio website. Not that I think it's a crucial aspect to my career, but I have always wanted a place to express my passion for my projects that I one day wanted to work on such as, ClayMatter.
